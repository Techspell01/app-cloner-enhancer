# Market Research Guidelines

## Research Strategy
When analyzing market viability for cloning an app:

### 1. Direct Competitors
- Search for "[app name]" + competitors
- Check ProductHunt, AlternativeTo.net, G2, Capterra
- Look for similar apps solving same problem

### 2. Demand Signals
- **Reddit**: Search r/[relevant subreddits] for discussions, complaints
- **HackerNews**: "whoishiring" style posts, Show HN, comments
- **Twitter/X**: User complaints, feature requests
- **App reviews**: Look for 1-3 star reviews for pain points
- **Google Trends**: Check search volume/trends

### 3. Pain Points to Find
- UI/UX complaints
- Missing features
- Pricing issues
- Performance problems
- Poor mobile experience
- Lack of certain integrations

### 4. Gap Analysis
Identify opportunities:
- **UX gaps**: Simpler flows, better onboarding
- **Feature gaps**: Missing functionality users want
- **Design gaps**: Outdated look, not mobile-friendly
- **Performance gaps**: Slow, bloated
- **Pricing gaps**: Freemium model opportunity

### 5. Market Analysis Output
Write `market-analysis.md` with:

```markdown
# Market Analysis: [App Name]

## Overview
[Brief description of app and problem it solves]

## Direct Competitors
- [Competitor 1] - [notes]
- [Competitor 2] - [notes]

## Market Demand
[Evidence of demand - discussions, reviews, trends]

## User Pain Points
- [Pain point 1 with source]
- [Pain point 2 with source]

## Opportunities/Gaps
- [Gap 1 - opportunity to improve]
- [Gap 2 - opportunity to improve]

## Differentiation Strategy
Our enhanced version will improve on:
1. [Improvement 1 - specific]
2. [Improvement 2 - specific]
3. [Improvement 3 - specific]

## Viability Assessment
- Demand: High/Medium/Low + reasoning
- Competition: High/Medium/Low + reasoning
- Feasibility: Easy/Medium/Hard + reasoning
- Overall: [Recommendation]
```

## Notes
- Focus on actionable insights, not academic reports
- Prioritize finding real user complaints you can fix
- Don't spend hours - 10-15 min of targeted research is enough
- If no clear gaps found, still improve UX/design/performance as baseline
